Drive Photos Bridge
— 00 MEDIA

An owner-operated bridge that copies photos and video clips from a selected Google Drive folder into the same owner's Google Photos library.

Google Drive→Google Apps Script→Google Photos

The owner exports media into the configured Drive folder. After those files reach Drive, a scheduled Google Apps Script job checks for new file versions and imports supported media into Google Photos using the original file bytes.

The bridge is designed to check every ten minutes. Transfers can span multiple runs and are subject to Google's service availability, processing, storage, file-size and daily usage limits.

This is a private tool for one owner's account. This website provides app information; it has no public sign-up, sign-in or media upload feature.

Data and control

The bridge reads the configured folder and its subfolders, adds media to Google Photos, and keeps private progress records in Google Drive. The owner can pause the schedule or revoke access through their Google Account.

Read the Privacy Policy for the information accessed and stored, and the Terms of Use for the tool's scope and operating conditions. The owner's support contact is shown on the Google authorization screen.
