Terms of Use

Personal scope

Drive Photos Bridge is a private automation for its owner's Google Account. It is not offered as a public service and this information website does not provide access to the owner's media or automation.

Operation

The owner controls the source folder, destination account, authorization and schedule. The tool copies supported media using the exported original bytes. With owner-approved cleanup enabled, it verifies the successful Google Photos copy, waits for video processing to finish, and moves only the unchanged source version from the configured folder to recoverable Google Drive Trash. It does not permanently delete files, empty Trash, or propagate a source deletion to Google Photos. Drive may sync source deletions back to the owner's computer. Changing a source file may result in importing a new version.

The automation is provided as-is for personal use. Transfers and playback depend on Google's services, account access, storage capacity, media support and usage limits. There is no guarantee of uninterrupted operation or a fixed transfer time. The owner remains responsible for retaining and checking their original files.

Google services and privacy

Use of Google Drive, Google Photos, Google Apps Script and the associated APIs remains subject to Google's applicable terms. This tool is independently operated and is not sponsored or endorsed by Google.

Data handling is described in the Privacy Policy. The owner can stop future imports by pausing the schedule or revoking authorization.
